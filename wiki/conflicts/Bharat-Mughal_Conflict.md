# <!--NAME-->Bharat-Mughal Conflict<!--NAME-->

The Bharat-Mughal Conflict is a currently ongoing conflict between [Bharat](47527ddf-a129-4063-829a-226b2d55d7ad_nation) and the [Mughal Empire](0bf0a1ac-f177-4da3-a759-1ddb1c3722b7_nation). It was initially started by the expansion into Northern India by what used to be known as Pakistan (now the Mughal Empire).  <br>

## History
It has been known that even since the day that \_Cao joined the server, Bharat and the Mughals have been at each others throats.    <br>
![Proof that since 7/8/25, theres been an issue between india and pakistan.](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1386079985961402472/1396302358715039754/image.png?ex=687d9768&is=687c45e8&hm=b2b607bf990053559eb4fd8f53d8ea2da1951040153799e123dc84bc837ecd60&)

On July 19th, 2025, at 8:22 ET, Mughal declared Bharat as an enemy and started their campaign of southern expansion into India. In response, a group of people, including Lucas of Brazil and AuraSwiss of Euroasia entered Ahmedabad, a colony of Mughal, to hopefully fight off \_Cao, the leader of the Mughal. In the end, Lucas was embarrassingly killed by AuraSwiss, and \_Cao was left to claim more Indian lands. Later in the day, \_Cao entered into [Sri Lanka](Sri_Lanka), the capital of Bharat, to annoy Gameking, the leader of Bharat. Lucas attempted to kill \_Cao, but was once again killed.
